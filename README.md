# Ronda

> Night-watch drills for small teams with no security team.

In Indonesian villages, *ronda* is the tradition of neighbors taking turns to guard the night. **Ronda** brings that idea to cybersecurity and safety: short, spooky, branching drills that teach small businesses and small teams what to do in the first hour of an incident, before it happens.

Built for **Kiroween 2026** (Skeleton Crew category) with [Kiro](https://kiro.dev).
